# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 29, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Metarlog ("the app", "we", "us") collects,
uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you create an account, we collect your **email address** and, if you
sign in with Google or Apple, the basic profile information those providers
share with us (such as your name and email address). This is used solely to
identify your account and sync your preferences.

### Location Data
With your permission, the app accesses your **device location** to center
the map on your current position and help you find nearby airports and
navigation points. Location data is used only within the app and is not
shared with third parties beyond the mapping service required to render the
map.

### Purchase Information
If you subscribe to Metarlog Premium, the purchase is processed entirely by
Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We only receive confirmation of
your subscription status (active/inactive) to unlock premium features — we
do not receive or store your payment details.

### Usage & Diagnostic Data
We use **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** (Google) to
understand general app usage patterns and to diagnose crashes. This data is
associated with an anonymous installation identifier, not your name or
email.

### Advertising
Free-tier users are shown ads served through **Google AdMob**. AdMob may use
device identifiers to deliver and measure ads. We do not use any tracking
technology that follows you across other companies' apps or websites, and
we do not request tracking permission for cross-app advertising.

### Device Security Check
On startup, the app performs a basic, local check for whether the device is
jailbroken or in developer mode. This check does not transmit any personal
data; it is used only to protect the integrity of the app.

## Data We Do Not Collect

We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera, and we do not
sell your personal information to third parties.

## Third-Party Services

Metarlog displays aviation weather and airport data sourced from third-party
providers (aviation weather services, airport data services, and a map
tile provider) and uses the following third-party services to operate:

- Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Authentication) — Google
- Google Sign-In — Google
- Sign in with Apple — Apple
- Google AdMob — Google
- Apple In-App Purchase / Google Play Billing

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how it
processes data on our behalf.

## Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from **Profile → Account Settings →
Delete Account**. This permanently removes your account and associated
preferences from our systems.

## Children's Privacy

Metarlog is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted
on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
