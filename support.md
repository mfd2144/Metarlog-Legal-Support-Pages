## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the weather data in Metarlog official?**
No. METAR/TAF and airport data in Metarlog are provided for general
informational and situational-awareness purposes only. They are not a
certified aviation weather source and must not be used as the sole basis
for flight planning, dispatch, or any operational decision. Always verify
current conditions with an official, certified aviation weather briefing
service before making any flight-related decision.

**How do I manage or cancel my subscription?**
Go to **Profile → Manage Subscription** in the app, which opens your
device's native subscription management page (App Store or Google Play).

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to **Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account** and confirm. This
permanently removes your account and preferences.
