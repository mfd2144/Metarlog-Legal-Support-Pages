# Delete Your Metarlog Account

You can permanently delete your Metarlog account and its associated data
directly from the app:

1. Open **Metarlog**
2. Go to **Profile → Account Settings**
3. Tap **Delete Account**
4. Type "delete" to confirm

## What gets deleted

Your account (email, sign-in credentials), premium/subscription status
flag, and saved preferences are permanently deleted from our systems
immediately after confirmation.

## What may be retained

- Apple/Google retain their own purchase and transaction records
  according to their own policies; we do not control this data.
- Anonymous, non-identifying diagnostic and analytics data (crash logs,
  usage statistics) may be retained for a limited period, but is not
  linked back to your deleted account.
